Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the way humans interact with technology.
From voice assistants like Alexa and Siri to self-driving cars, AI has become part of our everyday lives.

Natural Language Processing (NLP) enables machines to understand and generate human language.
For example, chatbots use NLP to provide instant customer support and automate tasks.

Cloud computing platforms such as AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud provide scalable infrastructure for deploying AI models.
AWS Bedrock allows developers to integrate Large Language Models (LLMs) like Claude and GPT without managing servers.

Databases play a crucial role in modern applications.
Relational databases like PostgreSQL and MySQL store structured data, while NoSQL databases like MongoDB and DynamoDB handle unstructured or flexible schemas.

Python has become the most popular programming language for AI and data science.
Frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, LangChain, and FastAPI help developers build intelligent and scalable applications.

Cybersecurity remains one of the biggest challenges in technology.
As systems become smarter, protecting data and ensuring user privacy is more important than ever.
